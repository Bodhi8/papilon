trademarks of Brian Curry. The Apache License 2.0 lets you use, change and sell the
code. It does not give you the names. This policy says what you may do with them.

The aim is simple: nobody should be misled about what they are getting or who made it.

## You do not need to ask

- Say truthfully that your work uses, is built on, is compatible with or is derived
  from Papilon, in plain text. For example: "built on Papilon", "works with Papilon".
- Redistribute an unmodified release under its own name.
- Use the names in articles, talks, courses, books and reviews about the software.
- Link to the project.

## Ask first

- Using a name or logo in the name of a product, service, company, package, domain,
  social account or user group.
- Using a logo anywhere other than to link to the project.
- Selling merchandise that carries a name or logo.
- Anything not covered here.

## Not allowed

- Distributing a modified version under the name Papilon, or under a name close enough
  to be confused with it. Rename your fork.
- Suggesting that Brian Curry or Vector1 Research endorses, sponsors or is affiliated
  with your product or service.
- Using the names in a way that is misleading or that disparages the project.

## Writing the names

Write "Papilon" and "PyCausalSim" as shown, as names, not as verbs or plurals. On first
use in a commercial setting, note that they are trademarks of Brian Curry.
