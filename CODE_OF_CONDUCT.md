# Code of conduct

This project is a place to do careful technical work.

- Be respectful. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Be honest about evidence: say what you tested and what you did not.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or publishing of other people's private
  information, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any project space.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do not
follow this. Report problems privately to brian@vector1.ai. Reports are kept
confidential.
